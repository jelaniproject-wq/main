# "Her." interview series: design status

_Last updated 13 Nov 2026_

## Status
- First designer did three deck rounds plus the first feature (Volume 1, "Golden"). Jelani decided to change designers after the first feature.
- Shareable brief for the next designer (Claude Doc): https://claude.ai/artifact/5tF38XRsWTr4B5Z6N77Y5E

## Why we're changing
- Typography craft: justified body copy with wide gaps, text wrapped around shapes, inconsistent sizes.
- Off-concept imagery: most slides led with bikini body crops instead of the woman, her world and her work.
- Templated: the same outline shape, blurred headline and dot grid on every slide.
- No physical or scanned elements, despite agreeing to add them.
- Spelling inconsistencies (brand name spelled three ways).

## Keep from the work so far
- Series concept: each woman picks one word that becomes her title. Interview structure: intro, getting to know her, in her own words, final reflection.
- Off-white paper base (Jelani prefers off-white to cream) and navy type.
- Accent colours as small pops and outlines, never large fills. Pull them from the moodboard (acid green, denim blue, plum), sitting between the soft brand palette and fluoro.
- Real scanned handwriting for quotes and names.
- Carousel length set by the content (Jelani is happy for it to take its natural form in the edit).

## Hiring for the next designer
- Look for editorial or print work (zines, magazines), specifically body-copy layouts, not only covers or social posts.
- Start with a paid first pass (cover plus three slides) before the full volume.
