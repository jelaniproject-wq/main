How Jelani sounds

Do

  • Write as "I". It's a founder-run brand.
  • A thoughtful journal entry meets editorial. Short, a little dreamy.
  • State facts plainly: what's on, how much, until when.
  • Scarcity only when it's true: limited run, limited numbers.
  • Ads can be bold. The site and organic posts stay understated. No emojis on the website.

Hard rules

  • Never mention the founding story or any cause in sale content.
  • Say "recycled" only about products that are.
  • Product names in Title Case: Scout, Isla, Darcy, Camo Hat.
  • Lead with in-stock styles (Scout, Camo Hat).
Icy + designer

Designer

Everything to design, highest priority first. If time runs short, cut from the bottom. Meta ad creatives are on Jelani's tab, but they reuse the key visual from here.

The idea

Black Friday as a page from Jelani's scrapbook. Analog, layered and handmade, like a ring-bound zine. It should feel like someone made it slowly. No black backgrounds, no blocky SALE type.

Colour

Soft paper off-white base. Dusty pink, plum, moss green and apricot in faded film tones. One hand-drawn accent line in acid yellow or red. Leopard print is fine as a texture.

Type, three voices

  • Handwriting: the Early Hours name, notes and scribbles
  • Typewriter: the facts (dates, %, code)
  • Editorial serif: headlines, used sparingly

Texture & photos

  • Vellum overlays, tape, paperclips, ring binding, star stickers, a ticket stub, hand-drawn outlines
  • Grainy, motion-blurred film. Prints taped onto paper
  • No girl-in-a-bikini-on-the-beach
