### Why a longer VIP (36h instead of 24h)
- **Signing up is worth more.** "A day and a half before everyone" is a stronger reason to join than "a few hours".
- **Room for a second VIP email.** Our launches peak in the first hour, then trickle. A Thursday-night reminder
  to VIPs who haven't bought gets a second peak from the people most likely to buy.
- **Easier on you.** You pack after work and on weekends. Orders spread over Wed–Thu instead of all landing on Friday.
- **Trade-off:** some sizes will sell out before the public sale. That's fine: it rewards the list, and it's
  honest scarcity for the public emails.
- Don't go past ~2 days. Longer than that and VIP just feels like the sale.

## 2. Voice for this campaign (from the founder interview)

The interview left the **sales tone** as a TODO ("not too urgent, but still needs to sell"). Here's a proposal.
If you're happy with it, I'll save it to CLAUDE.md.

**Proposed sales tone: generous, not urgent.**
- **Lead with thanks and first pick, not panic.** Black Friday is a thank-you to the people who've been here.
- **State the facts plainly:** what's on, how much, until when. One fact per line.
- **Scarcity only when it's true,** in our words: *limited run*, *limited numbers*, *our most loved*.
- **One urgency moment only:** the last-call email and banner. Nowhere else.
- **Ads can be bold** (big "UP TO 40% OFF"). Website and organic posts stay understated. No emojis on the website.

**Rules applied to Jas's copy**
| Interview rule | What changed |
|---|---|
| Founder speaks as **"I"** | Emails are written by you ("I want you to have first pick", "thank you for being here") |

## 4. Visual brief for designers (from the interview + rebrand deck)

- **No girl-in-a-bikini-on-the-beach.** Put the pieces in her life: records on a rug, a game of chess, a flower
  market, a bike with groceries, a film camera on a doorstep, an open-water swim, a festival.
- **Candid over perfect.** Mix clean campaign shots, grainy film and iPhone snaps in the same set.
- **Break up product shots** with text frames and close-ups (quotes, snippets, the 444 embroidery, print details).
- **Colour:** warm interiors with deep red and burgundy accents. Black Friday can be **burgundy-black instead of the usual black-and-red**, which marks us out from every other sale in the feed.
- **Type:** editorial and understated on the site and in emails. The percentage can be big in ads.
- **References:** Pinky and Kamal, BUCI, Mode Mischief Studios. Benchmark against Bersama, CMNTY GRDN and Casa La Sola, whose Black Friday is what ours sits next to.
- **Organic hero idea, in the style of your manifesto post:** *Black Friday, gently.* 1 Buy what you'll come back to.
  2 Choose something for someone you love. 3 Take your time (but they're limited runs). 4 Support small things
  made with care. 5 Then go outside.
