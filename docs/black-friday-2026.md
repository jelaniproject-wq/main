### Why a longer VIP (36h instead of 24h)
- **Signing up is worth more.** "A day and a half before everyone" is a stronger reason to join than "a few hours".
- **Room for a second VIP email.** Our launches peak in the first hour, then trickle. A Thursday-night reminder
  to VIPs who haven't bought gets a second peak from the people most likely to buy.
- **Easier on fulfilment.** Orders spread over Wed–Thu instead of all landing on Friday.
- **Trade-off:** some sizes will sell out before the public sale. That's fine: it rewards the list, and it's
  honest urgency for the public emails ("sizes are moving").
- Don't go past ~2 days. Longer than that and VIP just feels like the sale.

## 2. Jelani voice (PROVISIONAL, waiting on your interview answers)

*Your interview answers (story, voice, who it's for, where Jelani is heading) were never saved to this repo, and I
can't see past conversations. Until you send them, this is inferred from the live site's product descriptions and
past email names. That reflects Jelani today, not the future brand you're building. Once you send the answers, I'll
save them to CLAUDE.md and redo the copy below to match.*

**How Jelani sounds:** calm, warm, a little playful. Confident without shouting. Specific and visual: it names
colours, prints and details ("Leopard and hibiscus, together as they should be", "warm, earthy tones",
"embroidered cherries, star and 444 details"). Short sentences. Feels like a friend with good taste, not a sales floor.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Name the actual piece, colour or print | Generic hype ("DO NOT MISS THIS", "INSANE DEALS") |
| One caps headline, then normal-case copy underneath | Caps everywhere, exclamation marks everywhere |
| Use "angel" sparingly, as a wink (it ties to the 444 angel number on the Camo Hat) | "Angel" in every headline, which wears it out by email 2 |
| Light Aussie warmth ("sorted", "first in, best dressed") | Americanisms ("y'all", "deals deals deals") |
| Honest urgency ("sizes are moving") | Fake urgency you can't back up |

**Main change to Jas's copy:** "angel" now appears once per touchpoint at most. It's kept for the Angel Access name
and the final "last call". The shouty lines are gone. The copy now names real products instead of "your favourites".
