## Things to check before ordering
- **Ads:** Meta's ad rules restrict tobacco-related products. Lighter and cigarette cases may get ads rejected, so plan to sell them organically, as gifts with purchase, or bundled at events. Calling #2 a "trinket case" helps. *(Inferred; confirm against Meta's policy before running ads.)*
- **Lighters in Australia:** lighters have product-safety rules. Buy a compliant lighter and brand only the **case**, rather than branding the lighter itself.
- **Minimums:** engraved metal keyrings run roughly AU$2–4.50 each at ~250 units (a supplier's estimate). Small-batch metal cases often have a 50-piece minimum. Ask for a proof before production.
