# Restock planning: 14 Oct 2026

Sources: Shopify sales by variant (last 12 months), current stock, and Klaviyo back-in-stock signups.

## How this was worked out
- **Peak pace** is the average of the three best in-stock months: Jan, Jun and Jul 2026.
  - Aug–Sep 2026 sales were low partly because of stockouts (that's when back-in-stock signups piled up), so they understate demand.
- **Size split** is each size's share of the last 12 months of sales.
- **Months of cover** = stock now ÷ (peak pace × size share). It's a worst case. Oct–Nov 2025 were slower months.
- Summer (Dec–Feb) is the next peak. Any reorder needs to **land by early December**.
- **Unknown: supplier lead time.** Order date = run-out date minus lead time.
