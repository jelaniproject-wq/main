## What the business makes before overheads (estimate)
Net sales $93.7k − stock at ~$15/unit × 1,852 units ($27.8k, **assumed**) − payment fees ~3% ($3.4k) − Meta ($18.1k) ≈ **$44k a year, ~$3,700 a month**.
Not yet included: subscriptions and apps, design and content (Flo, Futur MGMT), packaging, accountant, postage above what customers pay. **Unknown. A guess is $1,000–1,500/month**, which would leave roughly **$2,200–2,700/month**.

## Shopify Capital
- Loan 1: about $15.7k repaid in total, ~$223 left. Loan 2: $7,622.80 ($6,800 received + $822.80 fee, a 12% flat fee), 17% of sales once loan 1 ends.
- Most of each repayment pays for stock already bought. The real cost is the fee: about $820 on this loan.
- **Not paying Capital would not by itself free up a wage**, because the stock would still need paying for. What stops a wage is the ~$2–2.7k/month left after costs (estimate), and the stockout gaps.
- The fee is fixed, so paying it off early probably doesn't save money (check the loan terms). Priority order for extra cash: Amex (interest) > Daniel (deadline) > Capital (runs itself).

## Plan
**Phase 1, now to ~Apr 2027: stop the cycle**
1. No new Capital loan. Loan 2 at 17% of ~$9.4k/month ≈ $1,600/month, so it's done in about 5 months (sooner with Black Friday and December).
2. Open a separate **stock account** and move **10% of every payout** there from Black Friday. Only use it for restocks.
3. Reorder a bestseller size when it has about 6 weeks of stock left, not when it's at zero.
4. Pay off Daniel ($4k by mid Dec), then the Amex.

**Phase 2, once Capital is done:** move the 17% that went to Capital into the stock account (total ~25%). That pays for the next restock up front, with no fee.

**Phase 3, pay yourself:** start with a small fixed amount (e.g. $500/month) once Capital and Daniel are cleared, and raise it as margin improves.

## Amex plan (added 28 Oct)
- Balance **$15,000, at its limit**. Started as business stock (socks, hats, men's shirts ~2–3 years ago) plus some personal spending. Paid from the business account. Interest rate not yet confirmed; ~21% p.a. assumed below.
- At ~21%, interest is about **$260/month**. A $142 minimum is less than that, so the balance can't come down on minimums. The $5k payment refilled because the card was used again, not because of the payment.

| Monthly payment (21% assumed) | Paid off in | Total interest |
|---|---|---|
| $142 (minimum) | never | ~$260/month, ongoing |
| $1,000 | ~18 months | ~$2,550 |
| $1,500 | ~11 months | ~$1,630 |
| $2,000 | ~8 months | ~$1,220 |

Steps:
1. Confirm the interest rate and minimum on the latest Amex statement.
2. Stop new spending on it: freeze the card in the Amex app, remove it from Apple Pay and saved payments, and move any subscriptions to ANZ.
3. Call the free **Small Business Debt Helpline (1800 413 828)** or **National Debt Helpline (1800 007 007)**. They can ask Amex for a hardship arrangement or lower rate, and compare a 0% balance transfer or a consolidation loan.
4. From **Jan 2027** (after Daniel is repaid), put the $2,000/month that went to Daniel towards the Amex. From ~Apr 2027 (Capital done) add more.
5. Sell old stock (Camo Hats: 90 left, ~2 a week; men's shirts: count and list or sell at a market) and put the proceeds on the Amex.

Debt priority is now: **Daniel (deadline) → Amex (highest interest) → Capital (fixed fee, pays itself).**
