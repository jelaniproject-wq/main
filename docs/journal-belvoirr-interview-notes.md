# Journal series: Belvoirr (photographer) interview notes

Interview answers received 7 Sep 2026. Covers Wren's shoot (Marseille villa) and Ava's shoot (grandmother's house near Bordeaux).
Still to come: Wren's and Ava's own answers, and photos (photographer sending via Instagram).

## Best material (in order)
1. **Ava at her "Moma's" (grandmother's) house near Bordeaux.** A personal, nostalgic place she has visited for years. Strongest emotional hook.
2. **Mosquito story (Wren's shoot).** Shoot kept stopping so everyone could put on mosquito spray. The only real behind-the-scenes moment, and it contrasts nicely with the calm images.
3. **Song of the trip: "Ouda".** Played at her 25th birthday dinner on the water in Marseille, and everyone noticed it fit the night. Vivid scene. Check the artist spelling (she wrote "Hamid Al Shari"; usually written Hamid El Shaeri).
4. **French heritage and Paris.** Explains why the shoots feel at home in France, not just a pretty backdrop.
5. **Directing philosophy.** "People can tell when your reactions are real." Brands with "a very clear world around them."
6. **Locations kept simple so the girls and the product stay the focus.** Natural tie-in to the product.
