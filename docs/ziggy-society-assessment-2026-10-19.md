## Verdict
**Worth joining as a founding brand, on a small, controlled scale.** There's no upfront cost and the 15% commission is locked in for life. The risks are operational: stock accuracy, returns and NZ postage. Read the Seller Agreement before signing.

## What ZIGGY is

- ZIGGY sells as JELANI's agent. JELANI stays the seller of record, ZIGGY never owns the stock, and JELANI ships in its own packaging.
- Shopify stores connect through Syncio. Stock syncs both ways and ZIGGY orders land in our Shopify tagged with the ZIGGY order number.

## Economics on a $100 product
| | Own site | ZIGGY |

| JELANI receives | ~$98 after payment fees (estimate) | $85 (85%) |
| Shipping | customer pays / our free-shipping rules | flat A$10 band (AU) or A$20 (NZ), paid to us even when ZIGGY offers free shipping |

- At our AOV ($107.53), commission is about **$16 per order**. That's far better than wholesale (usually ~50% of RRP) and likely cheaper than paid acquisition.
- The A$10 domestic band should cover a ~1kg tracked Sendle parcel (swimwear is light). For comparison, our own AU orders averaged ~$8 shipping charged per order over the last 12 months.
- **NZ is compulsory for Australian brands** (A$20 band). We currently charge NZ customers ~$20.70 per order on average (15 NZ orders, last 12 months), so the band roughly matches. Check the real tracked cost to NZ before signing.

## Risks and things to watch
1. **Stock accuracy.** Syncio sells off our Shopify numbers. Known problems (negative stock on Darcy Top and Cherry On Top, and Flowers In The Jungle Medium showing stock that wasn't on the shelf) would turn into oversells on a second channel. Fix these before listing.
2. **Our own customer emails.** ZIGGY orders flow into our Shopify, so they could trigger Shopify order confirmations, Klaviyo flows or Judge.me review requests to ZIGGY's customers. Check Syncio settings and exclude the ZIGGY tag in Klaviyo/Judge.me.
3. **Reporting.** ZIGGY orders show $0 shipping and are tagged. Report them as a separate channel, as with `collab` orders.
4. **Returns (Jelani's main objection).** ZIGGY doesn't accept change-of-mind returns (including the wrong size). Faults, misdescription and lost parcels are ours to cover, including return postage. **The guide doesn't say ZIGGY gives back its 15% commission on a refund.** It only waives commission on cancellations within 1 hour. If ZIGGY keeps it, every refund costs us the full price back to the customer when we only received 85%, so it's ~$15 worse per $100 than a refund on our own site, on top of postage both ways. For scale, our own-site refunds and returns over the last 12 months were $8,060 of $133,417 gross (~6%, all reasons). **Condition for joining:** commission is fully refunded on any refunded order or item, in writing in the Seller Agreement.
5. **Cash flow and counterparty.** We're paid monthly in arrears by the 7th, so money can be up to ~5 weeks late. ZIGGY is a brand-new startup holding the customer's money.
6. **Unproven traffic.** It hasn't launched yet, so the brand count, marketing budget and expected traffic are unknown.
7. **Price parity.** The ZIGGY price can't be higher than our site's. We set separate AUD and NZD prices. Any sale or price change has to be sent to ZIGGY too.
8. **Process rules.** The Vendor field must match the agreement exactly (`JELANI THE LABEL`, already consistent). Never delete a synced product in Shopify; use their change form.
