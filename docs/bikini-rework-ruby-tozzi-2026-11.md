# Bikini Rework: JELANI x Ruby Tozzi (Nov 2026)

**What:** Ruby Tozzi (freelance designer) is reworking Jelani's core swimwear collection.
**Source:** Dropbox folder shared by Jelani, 13 Nov 2026. It has two files:
- *JELANI x RUBY TOZZI* (16-page Canva brief deck, dated November 26')
- *JELANI x RT Core collection working PDF* (4 pages of Illustrator flats on croquis, front and back views)

## Project scope (from the deck)
- **Core styles:** develop and refine four signature swim sets
- **Fit and function:** better fit, comfort, support and coverage
- **Fabric and construction:** quality fabric bases, fabric techniques, clean finishes
- **Print, colour and trims:** the details that give the collection its individuality
- **Future development:** expanded swim and ready-to-wear (RTW)

## The four core sets
| Set | Based on | Her (persona) | Direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Scout** | Scout (existing) | The Original Jelani Girl / New York | **No major design changes.** Perfect the fit and update the fabric base. Build a core colour library plus seasonal prints. Later: trims, beads, toggles, metal hardware. This is the shape people recognise as Jelani. |
| **Micro** | Romy / Isla | The Bondi Girl | Refine the proportions of the small triangle top and the micro bottom. Clean and minimal but distinctive, with minimal tan lines. Core colours plus playful seasonal options. |
| **Bandeau & Mini Short** | Sav bandeau | The Explorer / Melbourne | Rework Sav into a secure, flattering bandeau with signature ties, toggles or elastic. New "shortie" bottom that also works off the beach. Textured fabrics and panel details. Opens the door to RTW (pairs with Jelani tees). |
| **Halter** | New | The Cool Traveller / Sydney | Longer, elongated halter with adjustable straps and good lift. Matching **wider tie brief**, not a thin string. Reference: sporty-chic vintage Prada girl. Beach to bar. |

## Design language
- **Core colour:** a play with **chartreuse and plum** (the flats show chocolate brown with chartreuse, and a silver/grey metallic)
- **Print inspiration:** red and blue tiger stripe, plum and chartreuse floral, sunset gradient, red feather/ikat, plaids and checks, blurred watercolour dots
- **Trim inspiration:** tassels, wooden and metal rings, beads, toggles, sporty logo elastic
- **Details in the flats:** ruched tie-front bandeau, shortie with contrast side panels and a front logo tab, contrast-trim V-neck tee (RTW)

## Notes for review (Claude, inferred)
- The four personas line up with the brand's *Bondi meets New York meets Melbourne* vibe.
- The **Micro / Bondi Girl** persona ("loves a tan", "always posing in front of the cam") leans more beach and influencer than the Jelani girl described in CLAUDE.md (introspective, curious, edgy). Content for this style should still avoid the "girl in a bikini on the beach" cliché.
- Any fabric claims from new fabric bases need to be confirmed before they go into copy (see CLAUDE.md > Claims).
- Product names: keep the existing names (Scout, Isla/Romy, Sav). The Halter needs a new girl's name.
