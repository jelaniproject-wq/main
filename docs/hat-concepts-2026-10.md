# JELANI hat concepts: brief for the maker call (22 Oct 2026)

Visual concept board (Design canvas): https://claude.ai/artifact/3wPuYKtvN6Fe2vgj79VukU

Reference style: the maker's hand-made patchwork "lids" (Caroline Fifteen). These are dad caps that are bleach-splattered
or hand-dyed with a distressed brim. Shapes cut from fabric are appliquéd on with a visible zig-zag stitch.
**Don't copy his own marks** (the "15", his 4-point star, "SPEED").

## Direction (from Jelani, 21 Oct)
- Vibe: **cool, edgy, festival girl**. Not pretty-coastal.
- **Coloured caps only.** No camo, no black. Bleach splatter on most of them (it's his signature).
- Stuck-on shapes must be **real shapes** (star, heart, flower, letters), not random scraps.
- JELANI is spelled in **individual cut-out letters**, not the oval bubble patch.
- **No JELANI on the front panel.** Keep fronts clean; put a small JELANI on the back above the strap (or a small side hit).
- Number idea = angel number **444** ("Crazy 444 You"), down the **side**, not big on the front.
- Jelani is based in Bondi, not Maroubra.

## Fabrics (Jelani's prints) and combos
- Red/blue plaid (hero, covers maroon + blue and red + blue)
- Pink/maroon check
- Yellow plaid (wildcard)
- New bikini colours: maroon + sky blue (offcuts)
- Combos: maroon + blue (hero), pink + green, pink + blue, red + blue

## Concepts
1. **Gingham Star.** Bleached maroon cap. Plaid patch cut in the **JELANI star** shape: a wonky, hand-drawn 5-point star with long uneven points and a rough edge (Jelani has the artwork file).
2. **Bikini Offcut.** Bleached pink cap. Maroon heart + sky blue flower cut from bikini offcuts. Needs interfacing on the stretch fabric.
3. **Crazy 444 You.** Bleached sky blue cap. Cut-out check letters running down the side.
4. **Bleach Flowers.** Maroon cap with stencil-bleached daisies (they come out pink).
5. **Moth Panel (CAD needed).** Red-wine canvas. Washed green organic panels crossing the seams, 2 rose panels with yellow outline, raised embroidery, pink scalloped visor edge.
6. **Stud Flowers.** Washed denim-blue cap. Silver stud flowers + a vine of small studs. Alt: dotted iris.
7. **Blob Spots.** Blush cap with airbrushed fuzzy blobs with spots. Can also be a printed fabric patch.

## What drives the price
Number of appliqué pieces (letters count individually) · placement count · bleach/dye work · embroidery digitising (Moth) ·
stud count · our supplied fabric vs his · run size / 1-of-1.

## Questions for the call (final list, 21 Oct)
**His process + materials**
- How do you choose the blank caps (brand, fabric, structured vs unstructured, adjustable sizing)? What base colours can we pick from?
- Where do you source patch fabrics, and how many colour/pattern options are there? Can we see swatches?
- Can we supply our own fabric (our printed plaid/check, bikini offcuts)? What works and what doesn't (stretch, thin, shiny)?
- How much control is there over the bleach (light vs heavy, placement)? How different will each cap look?

**Design input from him**
- From our concepts, which would stand out most and which would you simplify?
- What's achievable from your end and what isn't (small letters ~2.5 cm, organic panels, studs, airbrush, raised embroidery, CAD)?
- What's selling or getting the most reaction for you right now? Any techniques you've wanted to try?
- Do you do a mockup or sample before production? How many rounds of changes?

**Extras (festival)**
- UV/glow or neon thread? Reflective fabric? Print under the brim? Hidden pocket? Beaded strap or charm?
- Can you add our JELANI woven label, a care label and a numbered tag (01/44)?

**Timing**
- Sample turnaround? Time to make one cap, and how many per week?
- If we confirm this week, can ~44 (or 100) be ready by mid-November for festival season?

**Price + terms**
- Price per cap at each level (1 patch / 2–3 / lots of pieces)? What pushes the price up or down?
- Minimum order? Sample cost (credited back on the order?)? Deposit and payment terms? Shipping to Bondi?
- What happens with faulty caps or ones that don't match the sample?

**Quality + care**
- Wash/care instructions (we need these for the product page)? How long do the bleach and patches last?

**Brand + content**
- Are our designs exclusive to JELANI (not reused in his drops)?
- Would you like credit or a collab tag? Can we post your process?
- Can you film or photo the making (bleaching, stitching)? Great for Reels.

## Festival-season ideas (suggested 21 Oct, not yet decided)
**On the cap**
- UV-reactive or glow thread for the zig-zag stitching (glows under stage lights / UV tents)
- Reflective JELANI star patch (3M reflective fabric) that flashes in night photos
- Hidden print under the brim ("crazy 444 you" or a line of our own), seen in selfies
- Beaded back strap / JELANI star charm on the strap (kandi bracelet culture); custom name beads as an upsell
- Hidden zip stash pocket inside the sweatband (cash, card, key)
- One neon thread colour per cap (neon pink on maroon, etc.)

**How we sell it**
- Numbered 1-of-1s: 44 caps per drop, stitched "01/44" tag (ties to 444)
- Festival set: cap + matching bikini cut from the same fabric, bundle price
- Colourway names that feel like a festival day: Golden Hour, Main Stage, 3am, Sunrise Set (no festival trademarks)
- Timing: drop early–mid Nov so caps land before the NYE / January–February festivals; consider a pre-order to fund production
- Waitlist / early access through Klaviyo; launch on Instagram first (peak 7–8pm AEDT), email after
- Content: flash photos at night, UV-glow reel, gift a few to festival creators (tag the $0 orders `collab`)

**Ask the maker:** UV/glow thread? Reflective fabric? Hidden pocket? Woven numbered tags? Under-brim print?
