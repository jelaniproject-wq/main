# JELANI hat concepts: brief for the maker call (22 Oct 2026)

Visual concept board (Design canvas): https://claude.ai/artifact/3wPuYKtvN6Fe2vgj79VukU

Reference style: the maker's hand-made patchwork "lids" (Caroline Fifteen). These are dad caps that are bleach-splattered
or hand-dyed with a distressed brim. Shapes cut from fabric are appliquéd on with a visible zig-zag stitch.
**Don't copy his own marks** (the "15", his 4-point star, "SPEED").

## Direction (from Jelani, 21 Oct)
- Vibe: **cool, edgy, festival girl**. Not pretty-coastal.
- **Coloured caps only.** No camo, no black.
- **Bleach: undecided (22 Oct).** Tilly questioned it. Bleach splatter is his signature, so it may make ours look like his. Options: clean cap, light sun-faded wash, or bleach only on a limited 1-of-1 run. Ask him to sample one bleached + one clean.
- Stuck-on shapes must be **real shapes** (star, heart, flower, letters), not random scraps.
- JELANI is spelled in **individual cut-out letters**, not the oval bubble patch.
- **No JELANI on the front panel.** Keep fronts clean; put a small JELANI on the back above the strap (or a small side hit).
- Number idea = angel number **444** ("Crazy 444 You"), down the **side**, not big on the front.
- Jelani is based in Bondi, not Maroubra.
- Loves **contrast stitching** (topstitching, seams, eyelets and zig-zag in a colour that pops).
- **Only 4 weeks** (from 21 Oct, so caps needed by ~18 Nov).

## Dancer construction (22 Oct)
Two ways to make the dancing figures:
- **A. Cut-out:** each figure cut from fabric and stitched straight onto the cap. Cleanest look, but thin arms/legs are slow to cut and zig-zag, and can fray or lift.
- **B. Figure on a patch (recommended for dancers):** figure stitched/appliquéd onto a background patch, then only the simple outer shape is zig-zagged to the cap. Faster, stronger, figures read clearer. Patch idea: an organic pebble shape, like cave art on a stone (ties to the cave-painting style).
Letters (CRAZY 444 YOU) and the spiral are chunky, so they can stay cut-out. Ask him which method he prefers and what it does to price/time.

## More symbol references (22 Oct, from Jelani)
- **Two connected figures with stars** (one outline, one solid): best "connection" symbol. Solid figure = patch, outline figure = stitched line. Stars = JELANI star.
- **Spiky "eyes" with spirals inside:** pair them on the back above the strap.
- **Fine-line leaping dancers:** too thin to cut as patches. Make them as a stitched line drawing (thread drawing / free-motion or chain stitch).
- **Connected stars on a stem** (red): uses 4-point stars, which is close to his mark. Do it with **JELANI 5-point stars** as a "constellation" running down the side.
- **Hand-drawn red outline flower:** stitched outline in a thick contrast thread, or as the bleach stencil for Bleach Flowers.
Big idea: **mix solid patches with stitched line drawings** on the same cap. Ask him if he can thread-draw (free-motion / chain stitch).
- **Two reaching dancers with sparkles + dots** (chunky, blobby limbs): the strongest Side A option. Thick enough to cut as patches. Sparkles = JELANI stars, dots = small fabric dots or French knots.
- **Three dancers with spiral heads:** combines dance + spiral of nature. Solid body patches with the spiral stitched in contrast thread on the head.
- **Copyright:** these are found online (Pinterest / tattoos). Use as references only; get original JELANI artwork drawn, don't trace.
